# Registry scope

`registry.csv` begins the new Universal Portfolio family. Existing AOAE experiments, opportunity scans and `strategy-family-ledger.json` remain authoritative historical memory and are not deleted or silently reclassified. Migration into this registry is not complete.

Blank OOS/Sharpe/drawdown/turnover/ESS columns deliberately avoid manufacturing a single statistic across different synthetic worlds and seeds. Per-run net/gross metrics, trade legs and serial ESS are in the evidence JSON; OOS Sharpe and real-market effective sample size are not established. A RESEARCH row is not evidence of capital readiness. Graveyard stores falsified claims separately from implementation bugs.
